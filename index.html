PROPOSED SOLUTION

What is SignAR?

SignAR is a digital educational tool that combines real-time Filipino Sign Language recognition with augmented-reality-style visual feedback. A device camera captures hand gestures, computer vision extracts hand landmarks, the recognition layer identifies supported gestures, and the interface displays the sign and its meaning.

TECHNOLOGY

How the prototype works

  • Mobile camera: captures hand gestures in real time.
  • Computer vision: tracks hand landmarks and motion.
  • Machine-learning layer: combines adaptive landmark examples with the rule-based classifier.
  • AR visualization: places recognition feedback over the live camera.
  • Digital sign chart: lets learners study supported signs without using the camera.
TARGET USERS

Who SignAR supports

  • K–12 and secondary students learning Filipino Sign Language.
  • Teachers and educators supporting inclusive classroom communication.
  • Deaf students who may benefit when classmates and teachers understand more FSL.
  • Schools and institutions promoting accessible and inclusive learning.
EDUCATION + SOCIAL RELEVANCE

Why it matters

The proposal positions SignAR as an interactive learning and awareness tool intended to support inclusive education and reduce communication barriers between Deaf and non-Deaf individuals.
